FAQ

Common questions

What is debt review, and how do I know if I’m still under it?
Debt review (debt counselling) is a legal process that restructures your monthly debt payments and protects you from creditors while you pay. If a debt counsellor arranged reduced payments for you, or a “debt review” flag appears on your credit report, you are likely still under it. A free assessment will confirm your exact status.
How do I know if I qualify to exit debt review?
Eligibility depends on your circumstances — for example, whether your finances have recovered, the status of your accounts, and whether a clearance certificate can be issued. During the free assessment we review your situation and tell you honestly whether removal is possible.
Is my information kept confidential?
Yes. Every enquiry is private and confidential. We only use your details to assist with your enquiry, in line with POPIA, and we never share them without your consent.
How long does the removal process take?
Timelines vary depending on your circumstances and how quickly documents and credit bureau updates are processed. Once we assess your case we can give you a realistic time estimate for your situation.
Does this cost anything to start?
The initial assessment is free and comes with no obligation. If you choose to proceed, we’ll explain any costs clearly and upfront before you commit to anything.
What happens to my credit profile after removal?
Once removal is complete, the debt review flag is cleared from your credit profile with the bureaus. You can then apply for credit again, and we can guide you on practical steps to keep rebuilding your score.
